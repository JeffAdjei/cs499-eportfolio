01 PROFESSIONAL SELF-ASSESSMENT

Reflecting on the CS Journey

My technical growth, values, and readiness to contribute as a software engineer.

Completing the Computer Science program and developing this ePortfolio has allowed me to identify and demonstrate my strengths as a software engineer while refining my long-term professional goals. Throughout the program, I progressed from following structured instructions to independently analyzing systems, identifying weaknesses, and implementing meaningful improvements. This shift is most evident in the capstone enhancements, where I moved beyond simply building functional applications to designing scalable, efficient, and secure solutions.

From a technical perspective, I have developed strong capabilities in software design, algorithms, and databases. I improved application structure, validation, and modular design; I evaluated efficiency and redesigned systems to reduce redundancy; and I learned to push computation into the database layer instead of relying on inefficient in-memory processing. I also developed a stronger security mindset — incorporating validation, preventing unsafe operations, and considering how systems could be misused.

Together, the artifacts in this portfolio form a cohesive picture of my capabilities as a computer science professional. They demonstrate not only what I built, but how I evaluated existing systems, improved them, and aligned those improvements with real software development practices.

Outcomes Demonstrated

  • Collaborative, team-oriented engineering mindset
  • Clear written & visual technical communication
  • Software design & modular application structure
  • Algorithmic thinking for scalability & efficiency
  • Data-driven systems & secure query design
