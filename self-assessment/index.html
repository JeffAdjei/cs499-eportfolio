Professional Self-Assessment

Reflecting on the CS Journey

Jeffrey Adjei · Southern New Hampshire University · Computer Science

Completing the Computer Science program and developing this ePortfolio has allowed me to clearly identify and demonstrate my strengths as a software engineer while also refining my long-term professional goals. Throughout the program, I have progressed from following structured instructions to independently analyzing systems, identifying weaknesses, and implementing meaningful improvements. This transition is most evident in the capstone enhancements, where I moved beyond simply building functional applications to designing scalable, efficient, and secure solutions. These experiences have reinforced my goal of becoming a software engineering leader focused on building intelligent, data-driven systems that operate at scale.

Collaboration & Communication

One of the most important skills I developed during this program is the ability to collaborate and think in terms of team environments. While many assignments were completed individually, the structure of software engineering practices, such as modular design, documentation, and code clarity, forced me to think about how other engineers would interact with my work. This mindset is essential in real-world environments where collaboration drives decision-making and system evolution. In addition, I strengthened my ability to communicate technical concepts clearly through written narratives, code reviews, and structured documentation. These communication skills are critical when working with stakeholders who may not have technical backgrounds but rely on accurate and understandable insights.

Technical Growth

From a technical perspective, I have developed strong capabilities in software design, algorithms, and databases. In software engineering, I improved application structure, validation, and modular design, ensuring that systems are maintainable and scalable. In algorithms and data structures, I learned to evaluate efficiency and redesign systems to reduce redundancy and improve performance. In databases, I gained experience working with MongoDB and learned how to structure queries, optimize data retrieval, and push computation into the database layer instead of relying on inefficient in-memory processing. Additionally, I developed a stronger security mindset by incorporating validation, preventing unsafe operations, and considering how systems could be misused or exploited.

The Artifacts Together

The artifacts in this ePortfolio represent the integration of these skills. Each artifact demonstrates not only technical ability but also growth in problem-solving, system design, and professional thinking. The software engineering artifact shows my ability to structure applications effectively, the algorithms artifact demonstrates my understanding of efficiency and scalability, and the database artifact highlights my ability to design and optimize data-driven systems. Together, these artifacts form a cohesive representation of my capabilities as a computer science professional and provide concrete evidence of my readiness to contribute to real-world engineering teams.
